Routing inputs

Your business rules become explicit system rules.

⌖

Territory

Route by location, market, listing area, service line, or any structured geography available to the workflow.

◎

Ownership

Preserve agent-owned leads, treat ponds and groups differently, and define reassignment exceptions.

◷

Availability

Use working hours, schedules, eligibility, escalation order, and fallback paths before ringing.

↗

First answer wins

Ring multiple eligible agents while the lead stays on the line; assign the person who actually connects.

▣

Booking fallback

When no one answers, offer a calendar, schedule follow-up, or place the lead into an approved queue.

FUB

Write-back

Record the selected agent, transfer result, summary, stage, tags, and next action on the FUB record.

Handoff sequence

The lead should never wonder what happened next.

Each handoff has a defined success, no-answer, decline, voicemail, disconnected, and retry path.

  • AI summarizes motivation and qualification before or during handoff.
  • Eligible agents receive context rather than a blind call.
  • The lead remains in a coherent conversation if no human connects.
  • Assignment reflects the actual outcome rather than the attempted outcome.
