Implementation acceptance case study

Timothy’s eight-agent FUB team: from workflow map to production-ready AI follow-up.

A custom voice, SMS, listing, routing, and FUB-control system was built and exercised across the team’s core buyer and seller scenarios before rollout.

The operational problem

New leads, missed calls, listing requests, and handoffs needed one coordinated system.

The team used Follow Up Boss as its CRM, with leads arriving from Meta/StreetText and the AgentFire website/IDX. The desired outcome was not another agent dashboard. It was a behind-the-scenes system that could engage, qualify, and route while preserving FUB as the front end.

Inconsistent follow-up paths

Answered calls, missed calls, listing requests, and seller conversations each needed a defined next action.

Territory-aware handoff

Eight agents were organized into territory groups, with two to three eligible agents per territory.

Low activity visibility

A 30-day audit found only five of 312 leads with property activity recorded, exposing a tracking and recovery opportunity.

What was built

Voice, SMS, property search, FUB controls, and live transfer in one implementation.

☎

AI call qualification

Buyer and seller conversation paths with approved questions, outcomes, and escalation rules.

Missed-call recovery

Listings-only flow

Why the result matters

The outcome was an accepted operating system—not a generic chatbot demo.

Real failure paths were found

Agent-number identity, duplicate SMS, transfer behavior, and link formatting were exposed and corrected through scenario testing.

The CRM stayed central

The team could activate AI at the contact level and keep lead work anchored in Follow Up Boss.

The rollout had a controlled next step

The operator proposed a 50-lead production cohort rather than turning the system loose on the entire database at once.

Evidence note: This case study intentionally separates tested functional outcomes from business performance. A 50-lead cohort was proposed in the project record, but no verified cohort conversion, appointment, or revenue result was available to publish here.
