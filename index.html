Frequently asked

What FUB teams usually need to know.

How is this different from FUB Smart Messages and Action Plans?

Smart Messages help agents draft replies. Action Plans organize sequences, tasks, emails, tags, and stages. This implementation adds an autonomous conversational layer that calls or texts, captures qualification, continues follow-up, and writes the result back into FUB before a human takes over.

Will every AI text appear inside Follow Up Boss?

The system is designed so inbound and outbound AI texts can be recorded on the FUB person record alongside summaries, fields, notes, tags, stages, and handoff status.

Does it respect ponds, groups, owners, and territories?

Yes. We first document your ownership and eligibility rules, then apply those rules to transfers, notifications, and assignments. Agent-owned leads can remain with their current owner.

Do we have to replace our FUB phone number?

Usually not. Messaging and voice architecture are mapped around your current setup. Any number-porting requirement is identified before build rather than introduced after launch.

Can it work aged or imported leads?

Yes, when consent, brokerage policy, carrier rules, suppressions, and opt-outs permit outreach. We define eligible segments before activating re-engagement.

What do we own?

Your accounts, API keys, workflows, configuration documentation, and operational handoff. You pay raw telephony, model, and hosting costs directly rather than renting our software indefinitely.
